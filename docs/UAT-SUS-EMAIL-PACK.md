## 6) Pre-Send Validation Checklist

Before sending any email, verify:

1. Correct role template is used (`Student/Requester`, `PTCF Staff`, `System Admin`).
2. All placeholders are replaced:
   - `[Participant Name]`, `[Role]`, `[Login Email]`, `[Temporary Password]`, `[UAT Link]`, `[SUS Link]`, `[Support Contact]`, deadline/time window fields.
3. UAT and SUS links open correctly.
4. Deadline is shown with absolute date/time and timezone:
   - `May 20, 2026 5:00 PM Asia/Manila`
5. SUS instruction appears only after UAT task instruction.
6. Reminder schedule is set:
   - T-3 days
   - T-1 day
   - Deadline day
